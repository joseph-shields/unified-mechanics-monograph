**How a sentence, asked to describe itself, becomes a universe.**

One idea, told twice. A monograph unfolds it as a story. A companion standard holds it to the record.

Unified Mechanics derives physical structure from a single starting act: a system describing itself. That act forces two channels, the two channels force the golden proportion (φ² = φ + 1), the proportion forces a retained fraction r = 1/(2φ), and the fraction squares into three weights that exhaust the whole. From there the framework unfolds, with no free parameter anywhere in any map, through the field, the traversal price r³, the E8 carrier, the shape of quantum mechanics, the Lorentzian form of spacetime, observation, and a graded cosmogony. The claim is not "here is the true theory of everything." The claim is stronger in one direction and humbler in another: here is a language, watch how far it unfolds, and watch it show not only that it predicts but why anything predicts at all.

## A word on grades

Every claim carries a grade, and the grades are never quietly mixed. **Proved** follows from stated assumptions. **Conditional** follows once a named condition is granted. **Proposed** is a physical reading offered for calculation and falsification. **Postulate** is a starting commitment. **Open** marks a question left deliberately standing. Keeping these apart is the difference between a framework and a mood.

## Falsification

One sentence governs every test. If a stationary, self-holding process, counted by a convention declared before the count, returns a standing ratio that is not r within its stated error, the physical reading is wrong. And one standing rule governs every map: no free parameters, because a knob in the map can manufacture any agreement and therefore certifies none.
