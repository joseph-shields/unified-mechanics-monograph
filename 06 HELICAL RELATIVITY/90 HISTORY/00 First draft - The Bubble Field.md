# PART ONE. In plain terms

## There is no sheet

The rubber sheet is the picture everybody is given, and it has a hole in it that is usually
passed over quickly. A sheet is a thing. It is made of something, it has a tension, and it
sits in a room. When you put the ball on it, the sheet sags because there is a *floor*
underneath pulling the ball down. The picture that is supposed to explain gravity has
gravity hidden inside it.

So take the sheet away and put nothing back in its place except the field itself.

Space is a sea of tiny bubbles. Each bubble is a cell of least action: the smallest
complete unit of happening. Not a particle, not a piece of matter, just one indivisible
quantum of *something occurring*. The bubbles are all there is. There is no surface for
them to sit on and nothing underneath.

## What happens when you drop a ball in

The bubbles near the ball get heavier and they get pulled toward it. And here is the part
that does the work: the bubbles pull the bubbles. A bubble that has been drawn inward drags
its neighbours in with it, and those drag theirs. The pull propagates through the sea
without anything transmitting it, because the sea is what is being pulled.

So the region near a mass is *crowded*. More bubbles are packed into the same coordinate
volume than belong there, and each of them is larger than it should be.

That crowding is the whole of gravity. There is no sagging, because there is nothing to
sag. There is only a place where the bubbles are packed in too tight.

## Two handles, and why there must be exactly two

A bubble is characterised by two numbers and no more. How many of them there are per unit
volume, and how big each one is. Density and size.

They have to be independent, and this is not a stylistic choice, it is forced. If density
and size were locked together, so that squeezing more bubbles in automatically made each
one smaller by exactly the compensating amount, then the bubbles would always fill space
perfectly, the crowding would always be zero, and nothing could ever curve. Space would be
flat everywhere forever.

**Curvature exists precisely because the two handles can come apart.** Gravity is the
failure of the bubbles to tile space neatly.

## The lock

One more thing, and it is the thing that turns the picture into a theory.

A bubble holds one quantum of action. Everywhere. Always. That is not an additional
assumption; it is what makes a bubble a bubble rather than an arbitrary blob. If a cell
could hold half a quantum or three quanta, it was never a least-action cell in the first
place.

Action is energy multiplied by duration. So if a bubble sits deep in a gravitational well
and its energy drops, its duration has to rise to compensate. Longer duration means fewer
cycles per second, and a clock is nothing but a bubble counting its own cycles, so **clocks
run slow near mass.** That is gravitational time dilation, and it fell out of arithmetic
rather than being installed.

The same lock applied to the space direction says that the stretching of length and the
slowing of time are reciprocal: whatever factor one goes up by, the other goes down by. And
that single reciprocity, written out, *is* the Schwarzschild solution. Exactly, not
approximately.

## The number this predicts

Take the measured vacuum energy of space. That is the one thing assumed. It is the energy
density of the undisturbed bubble sea, so it equals the energy of one bubble divided by the
volume of one bubble. Combine that with the action lock and the speed of light, and every
property of a bubble is determined with nothing left to adjust:

| | |
|---|---|
| **duration** | 2.93 × 10⁻¹³ seconds |
| **size** | **87.8 microns** |
| **energy** | 2.25 milli-electronvolts |
| **number density** | 1.48 × 10¹² per cubic metre |

Eighty-eight microns. About the width of a human hair. Not the Planck length, not anything
microscopic in the usual sense: a scale you could see with a decent microscope.

That is the field's most exposed claim and the easiest one to go after. Gravity below a
tenth of a millimetre should show the grain of the bubbles, and torsion balance experiments
are working at exactly that scale now.

## Why the vacuum energy behaves the way it does

Take a region of the bubble sea and stretch it. What happens to its energy density?

Nothing. The bubbles have fixed action, so they do not thin out or lose energy. The
stretched region simply contains more of them, each exactly as it was. So the energy
density of the vacuum does not dilute when space expands.

An energy density that stays constant under expansion has pressure equal to minus its
density. That is what cosmologists write as w = −1, and it is the defining property of dark
energy. The one thing assumed here arrives with its own equation of state already attached,
because "fixed action per cell" and "does not dilute" are the same sentence.

## Gravity between two things

This is where the bubble field departs most sharply from the standard framing.

General relativity begins by asking for the stress-energy tensor of an object, and then
computes what that object does to geometry. The bubble field never does this. There is no
"the field of the Sun" waiting for the Earth to be dropped into it. There is one bubble
sea, and both bodies are simply things that make it crowded.

The attraction between them is not a force sent from one to the other. Each body sits in
the total crowding and drifts down its gradient. The field is the *relationship*, not a
property of either object.

Computed for the Sun and the Earth, the gradient of the crowding gives 3.541666 × 10²²
newtons. Newton's law gives 3.541666 × 10²² newtons. They agree to seven figures, and the
bubble version is the one that also gets the relativistic corrections right.

## Where the other forces sit

The two handles are not symmetric, and the asymmetry is suggestive.

**Density has no ceiling.** You can always crowd more bubbles into a region. So a force
carried by density never saturates, never shuts off, and simply keeps adding as you add
more source. Long range, universal, always attractive. That is gravity, and it is gravity
because density is the unbounded handle.

**Size has a floor.** A bubble cannot shrink below one quantum of action, because below
that it is not a bubble. So a force carried by bubble size behaves normally until the floor
is reached, and then it cannot give any further.

A force whose response saturates and then refuses to yield is a force whose energy grows
without limit as you pull. That is confinement, and confinement is the signature property
of the strong force.

So the proposal is that the forces are one field seen at different densities: gravity is
the bubble sea responding with its unbounded handle, and the strong force is the same sea
with its bounded handle jammed against the floor.

# PART TWO. The derivation

## Definitions

A **bubble** is a causal cell carrying one quantum of action:

```
    ε · τ = ℏ                 one quantum of action
    a = c τ                   it is a causal cell
```

with ε the bubble energy, τ its duration, a its size. The field is described by two scalar
functions of position, the number density n(x) and the size a(x).

## The bubble from the vacuum energy

The undisturbed sea has energy density ρ_Λ = ε₀ n₀, and bubbles at baseline fill space, so
n₀ = 1/a₀³. Substituting the two defining relations:

```
    ρ_Λ = ε₀ / a₀³ = (ℏ/τ₀) / (cτ₀)³ = ℏ / (c³ τ₀⁴)
```

which inverts to

```
    τ₀ = ( ℏ / ρ_Λ c³ )^(1/4)
```

With Planck 2018 values, H₀ = 67.66 km/s/Mpc and Ω_Λ = 0.6889, giving ρ_Λ = 5.3240 × 10⁻¹⁰
J/m³:

| quantity | expression | value |
|---|---|---|
| duration | (ℏ / ρ_Λ c³)^(1/4) | 2.9282 × 10⁻¹³ s |
| size | c τ₀ | 8.7784 × 10⁻⁵ m = **87.8 μm** |
| energy | ℏ / τ₀ | 3.6015 × 10⁻²² J = 2.248 meV |
| density | 1 / a₀³ | 1.4783 × 10¹² m⁻³ |

The consistency check ε₀ n₀ = ρ_Λ holds to machine precision, as it must, since the system
is one equation in one unknown.

The combination ρ_Λ^(−1/4) landing near a tenth of a millimetre is a known numerical
coincidence in the cosmological literature. What this construction adds is that it is not a
coincidence but the *length of an object*, whose size, duration and energy are locked to one
another by a single quantum of action.

## Equation of state

Expand a coordinate volume V by a factor s. Bubble action is fixed, so bubble energy and
size are unchanged and the region simply contains sN bubbles instead of N. Then

```
    E = ε₀ n₀ V s        ρ = E / (Vs) = ε₀ n₀        independent of s
    p = −dE/dV = −ρ      so   w = −1
```

exactly, for the baseline sea.

## The metric is the packing fraction

Proper length is measured by counting bubbles along a path and multiplying by their size.
For a coordinate length L the count is n^(1/3) L and the proper length is n^(1/3) L a, so

```
    Ψ ≡ proper length / coordinate length = (n/n₀)^(1/3) (a/a₀)

    Ψ³ = n a³ / (n₀ a₀³)
```

Ψ³ is the packing fraction: the fraction of coordinate volume the bubbles actually occupy.
Flat space is Ψ = 1. Curvature is Ψ ≠ 1, that is, **overcrowding**.

Note that n a³ = constant would force Ψ ≡ 1 identically. The independence of the two
handles is therefore a precondition for curvature existing at all, not an extra freedom.

## The time factor

A bubble at potential Φ has energy ε = ε₀(1 + Φ/c²), with Φ negative going inward, which in
this framework is forward evolution and not a reversal. Fixed action gives τ = ℏ/ε, so

```
    τ = τ₀ / (1 + Φ/c²)        duration rises going inward
```

A clock counts bubble cycles, so its rate relative to coordinate time is

```
    T ≡ √(−g_tt) = τ₀/τ = 1 + Φ/c²
```

which is gravitational time dilation with the correct sign and the correct Newtonian limit.

## The action lock, and Schwarzschild

The bubble's cell in the time-radial plane has extent (duration) × (radial length), and the
action it holds is that product. Constant action per bubble therefore requires

```
    T · Ψ_radial = 1        equivalently        g_tt g_rr = −1
```

Writing g_tt = −f(r) with f fixed by the Newtonian limit, f = 1 − 2GM/rc², the lock gives
g_rr = 1/f and the line element

```
    ds² = −(1 − 2GM/rc²) c²dt² + (1 − 2GM/rc²)⁻¹ dr² + r² dΩ²
```

which is the Schwarzschild vacuum, exactly and to all orders. No field equation was solved.
The content is entirely in the statement that every bubble holds the same action.

## The tests

To confirm the lock is load bearing rather than decorative, photon and planetary orbits
were integrated numerically in two cases:

- **Case A**, time dilation only: bubbles slow clocks but do not crowd, g_rr = 1.
- **Case B**, time dilation with the packing lock: g_tt g_rr = −1.

Orbit constants were fixed from the two turning points rather than from the Newtonian
relation between (a, e) and (E, L), since that relation is case dependent and would
otherwise inject a spurious shift of the same order as the effect being measured.

**Deflection of light at the solar limb:**

| case | result | closed form |
|---|---|---|
| A, time dilation only | 0.87563″ | 2GM/bc² = 0.87562″ |
| **B, with the packing lock** | **1.75125″** | 4GM/bc² = 1.75124″ |
| measured (VLBI) | 1.7512″ | |

Case B error: +0.003 per cent. Ratio B/A = 1.999999.

**Perihelion precession of Mercury:**

| case | result |
|---|---|
| A, time dilation only | 28.655″ per century |
| **B, with the packing lock** | **42.982″ per century** |
| measured residual | 42.98 ± 0.04″ per century |

Case B error: +0.005 per cent.

Dropping the packing lock produces exactly one half of the light deflection and exactly two
thirds of the precession, which are the classic near-miss values. **The famous factor of two
in light bending is the bubbles crowding.**

## The relational two-body computation

The overcrowding field is built from both bodies at once,

```
    O(x) = Ψ³ − 1 = 3ε + 3ε² + ε³ ,     ε(x) = −Φ_total(x)/c²
```

with Φ_total the combined potential of Sun and Earth. No stress-energy tensor of either body
is constructed. The force is the gradient of the crowding:

| | |
|---|---|
| dO/dr at Earth's orbit | −1.979492 × 10⁻¹⁹ per metre |
| force from the gradient | 3.541666 × 10²² N inward |
| Newton | 3.541666 × 10²² N inward |
| ratio | 1.0000001 |

The gradient is negative, meaning the crowding falls off outward and the gradient points
inward, which under this framework's convention is forward evolution.

*Numerical note.* The expanded form 3ε + 3ε² + ε³ is essential. Computing (1+ε)³ − 1
directly, with ε of order 10⁻⁸, loses eight significant figures to cancellation, which is
the entire size of the gradient being measured.

## The forces, structurally

The two handles differ in one respect that is not a matter of interpretation:

```
    n  is unbounded above          →  a density force is long range, universal, additive
    a  is bounded below by a_min   →  a size force saturates at the floor
```

The floor exists because a bubble holding less than one quantum of action is not a bubble.
A response that saturates and then cannot yield further is one whose energy rises without
bound as separation increases, which is a linear potential, which is confinement.

# The programme

**One. Check the 88 microns against the existing data.** Eöt-Wash and successors have
constrained deviations from the inverse square law down to tens of microns. Those bounds
are set for Yukawa-type deviations, and a granularity signature is a different observable,
so the comparison has to be done properly rather than assumed either way. This is the first
thing to do because it is the fastest route to either a confirmation or a correction.

**Two. Impose the floor and look for the linear potential.** Solve for a(x) near a source
subject to a ≥ a_min. If the response goes linear in separation once the floor is reached,
the strong force is this same field with its bounded handle jammed, and the claim that the
forces are one thing at different densities becomes a computation rather than a proposal.

**Three. The source sector.** What is derived here is the vacuum. The remaining step is the
statement of how matter sets n and a *inside* itself, which is what replaces the field
equation. The two-body result shows the relational form this should take: crowding built
from all sources at once, never from one object's tensor.

**Four. Join it to the boundary field.** The companion algebraic result is that the
boundary field's fixed point is intrinsically the inward branch, the minus sign being forced
by the action rather than chosen. Inward, in this framework, is forward. That is the same
sentence as "the mass pulls the bubbles toward it", and the two lines should be written as
one.
