# Start Here

Unified Mechanics is presented in this edition as a theory of everything: a common structural layer beneath general relativity, quantum field theory, and quantum mechanics.

Begin with `Unified_Mechanics_Current_Scientific_Position.pdf`. It explains the authority order, the continuous derivational chain, the results completed by the Nine-Step Closure, and the small number of calculations and prospective measurements that remain active.

For the full theory, read `Unified_Mechanics_Complete_Series.pdf`. The five standalone volumes provide the same chronological architecture with volume-specific pagination. The omnibus is an integrated edition, not a page-for-page concatenation.

Then read `Unified_Mechanics_Derivation_Programme.pdf` for the conventional unification map and `Unified Mechanics - The Nine-Step Closure.pdf` for the later formal, computational, and empirical closure layer.

Machine inventories, historical layouts, and source-coverage tables are stored under `audit/`. They are provenance and verification materials, not competing scientific summaries.
