# Unified Mechanics

Four PDFs.

**A World of Distinctions.** The monograph, complete at twenty-seven movements. How a sentence, asked to describe itself, becomes a universe: the carrier it has to be written on, the descent that leaves only what can still be read, what survives that descent as space and as a clock, the shape spacetime is forced into, where gravity comes from, what it costs to be a thing at all, and a record that refuses to be overwritten. Read this one first, start to finish.

**Universopedia, The Observational Standard.** The companion reference: what a record is, what can honestly be said about a system, and where established science ends and the framework's added layer begins.

**Systems of Interpretation.** The record of throwing the framework at real systems: where it grips, and where it slides off exactly the regimes its own grammar forbids. The demonstration that the prior can fail, which is the strongest thing a prior can be.

**The Formal Register.** The audit surface: one notation, numbered axioms, definitions and results, every constant in closed form, and the dependency graph drawn explicitly and shown to be acyclic. For checking rather than reading.

Every claim carries a grade: proved, conditional, proposed, postulate, open, or dissolved. They are never mixed, and every open question states why it is open.

Joseph Shields, 2026. Corpus Edition, Fourth Printing.
