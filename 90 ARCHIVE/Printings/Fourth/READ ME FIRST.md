# Unified Mechanics
### The Corpus · 2026 · Fourth Printing

One idea, told three ways. A monograph unfolds it as a story. A standard holds it to the measured record. A register states it so it can be checked.

## What is here

**The Monograph. A World of Distinctions.**
How a sentence, asked to describe itself, becomes a universe. Twenty-seven movements, one continuous line of reasoning, complete. It runs from the single act of self-description through the two channels that forces, the golden proportion, the three weights, the field, the price of crossing a body and the ladder of stable systems; then to the carrier the whole account has to be written on, and the descent that leaves only what can still be read; then to what survives that descent as space, as a local clock, and as the closure among clocks, and the shape spacetime is forced into by their opposite roles; then onward through why an equation has the shape it has, what an action is, how constraint makes probability meaningful, why the quantum keeps its alternatives open, how identity is carried and where gravity comes from, what it costs to be a thing at all, and what a watcher needs before a fact can be called one; and at the far end an ocean, the architecture of one world, the luminous wound, and the record that refuses to be overwritten. Each step follows from the last, and wherever the book says forced, it shows the forcing. Read this first, straight through.

**The Standard. Universopedia, The Observational Standard.**
Not a monograph but a reference: an open, source-traceable account of how physical systems become describable, and of what may honestly be said about them. It closes with **Systems of Interpretation**, the record of throwing the framework at real systems to see where it grips and where it slides off. That part is a record, not a verdict, and it says so plainly.

**The Register. The Formal Register.**
Not a story and not a reference but an audit surface: one notation, numbered axioms, numbered definitions, every result with its hypotheses and its proof or citation, a certified numerical register, and the dependency graph drawn explicitly. The graph is acyclic, which is the checkable form of the Monograph's promise that nothing is slipped in by hand. A reader who wants to verify the chain rather than read it should start and finish here.

## How to read it

Read the Monograph as a story, beginning to end. Turn to the Standard when a claim makes you want to see the record behind it, and to the Papers as Issued when a movement makes you want the full derivation. Turn to the Register when you want to stop being persuaded and start checking. The story earns its ideas; the standard keeps them accountable; the papers carry the rigour; the register carries the shape.

## A word on grades

Every claim carries a grade, and the grades are never quietly mixed. **Proved** follows from stated assumptions. **Conditional** follows once a named condition is granted. **Proposed** is a physical reading offered for calculation and falsification. **Postulate** is a starting commitment. **Open** marks a question left standing, and in this printing every open question also states why it is open. **Dissolved** marks a question shown to be a convention rather than a physical unknown. Keeping these apart is the difference between a framework and a mood.

## What the framework still owes

One representation: the concrete form of the decay operator on the carrier. One number that follows from it: that the surviving spatial modes are three. One catalogue that follows from both: which gauge structure, which matter, how many families, what masses. One combinatorial lemma: that a complete carrier closure passes each of the 240 roots exactly once. And three measurements: the expansion integral, the audited pipeline count, and a live reading of r in a running system.

That is the entire list, it sits in one place at the end of the Monograph, and every item on it says why it is owed. The framework does not object to open questions. It objects to questions left open because nobody looked.

## What this edition changed

This printing absorbs the forward closure. The carrier no longer sits at the end of the mathematical section as a curiosity; it comes first, and physics is what survives its descent. Four movements are new or rewritten around that reversal, and three long-standing stipulations became derivations: the carrier's evenness now descends from the same two-sided reading that fixed the factor of two in r, so E8 is conditional on one demand rather than three; the root three of the covolume is derived as the area of the smallest closed carrier cell; and the surviving clock and closure modes are each shown to be single, from the arrow and from the one-world result. The boundary weight is identified with the interference term of a quantum probability, exactly and to the last decimal, which makes Movement Five and the quantum reconstruction one result at two depths. Gravity becomes the surviving part of carrier nonclosure, and gauge, matter and mass become three ways an operation can stand with one operator. The two open ledgers the corpus was carrying are merged into one. The Standard is unchanged. The Third Printing sits in Archive, byte-exact, its own frozen object.
