# Freeze Manifest
### Unified Mechanics · The Corpus · 2026 · Fourth Printing

This printing absorbs the forward closure into the Monograph and merges the corpus onto one ledger. No proved result of any earlier printing is retracted. What changed is depth of reading, not content: the same mathematics, read with machinery the earlier printings did not have. Every supersession is named below rather than left silent.

The Monograph grows from twenty-three movements to twenty-seven. Movements One to Eight carry over verbatim apart from three sanctioned edits: the edition line, the promise paragraph of Movement One which now names the book's full reach, and a sentence added to Movement Two stating the originating axiom in the open. Movement Nine is rewritten. Movements Ten, Eleven and Twelve are new. Movement Nineteen is rewritten and Movement Twenty is new. Movement Twenty-Seven is rewritten around the single ledger. All remaining movements carry over with their numbering advanced.

## The interpretive reversal

Physical dimensions are not appended to an eight-dimensional carrier and nothing is compactified. The carrier comes first, and what is called the physical world is the part of it that remains readable after a system-preserving descent. The physical algebra is what the decay operator leaves alone. This governs Movements Nine through Twenty and supersedes any earlier reading in which the carrier appeared after the physics rather than beneath it.

## Closed since the Third Printing

- **Carrier evenness.** Previously a stipulation, now derived: a relation read outward and back counts its self-pairing twice, so carrier norms land on the even integers. Positive-definiteness follows from the founding move, since an element pairing to zero with itself is a distinction making no difference to itself. E8 is therefore CONDITIONAL on one demand, the two-sided reading, rather than on three separate stipulations.
- **Root transcription.** Closed by the quantised crossing lemma of Paper 18, which supplies exactly the exactness and multiplicativity the carrier triangle needed. The corpus already held this proof and had not connected it.
- **The root three.** Derived as the area of the parallelogram spanned by two roots at the lattice angle, with the closed triangle at half of it. Movement Nine previously asserted it as the local carrier covolume and Movement Ten carried it in the vacuum readout. PROVED.
- **Clock and closure ranks.** Each shown to be single: the clock from the additivity of the arrow, since two independent orders of accumulation would be two records and therefore two systems; the closure from the one-world result. PROVED.
- **Lorentzian signature.** CONDITIONAL, now with two independent witnesses: the opposite closure roles of spatial opening and causal completion, and the order-preservation route through Alexandrov and Zeeman carried in Paper 19.
- **Hilbert structure, norm rule and Born pricing.** Closed by citation to Movements Seventeen and Eighteen, which derive them from named theorems. The forward-closure drafting notes recorded these as outstanding; they were already held.
- **The carrier-to-physical scale.** DISSOLVED as a unit convention, by the same argument that dissolved the finite readout rate in Paper 18. With it goes the last quantity that resembled a free parameter.

## New, and graded

- The system-preserving descent, its five conditions, and the identification of the physical algebra as the kernel of the decay generator. PROVED given the conditions.
- The distinction between the minimal decay generator, which is idempotent and suffices for the closure theorem, and the general positive generator, which the graded descent and the mass spectrum require. Recorded because an idempotent generator admits only two values and could not carry a mass spectrum; stating it is the difference between a derivation and an assumption.
- Space, local clock and clock closure as the surviving modes, with the fifth retained as closure data and excluded from traversable directions. PROVED for two of the three ranks; the spatial rank is OPEN with its reason and its constraint stated.
- Light identified as the boundary channel wearing coordinates, and the wound account and the cluster account of light reconciled explicitly as one thing at two depths. PROPOSED, with the reconciliation stated so the corpus does not carry two apparently different lights.
- Gravity as the surviving part of complete carrier nonclosure, and the three weights landing on the topological, dynamical and volume terms of the gravitational account, with local gravity living in the boundary cross-term. PROVED for the three-term structure; PROPOSED for the identifications. Supersedes the earlier reading of gravity as the common closure of all field accounts, which was the correct intuition without the mechanism.
- Gauge, matter and mass sorted by how a carrier operation stands with the decay operator: commuting, off-diagonal, and failing to commute respectively. PROVED given the general generator; the concrete content is OPEN and tagged to the calculation programme.
- **The boundary weight is the interference term.** At primitive magnitudes u and r the expansion of a squared sum gives 0.477458, 0.427051 and 0.095492, summing to one: the three weights exactly. PROVED as arithmetic; CONDITIONAL as physics on the primitive magnitudes being u and r, which is named rather than buried. This makes Movement Five and the quantum reconstruction one result at two depths.

## The Formal Register

This printing adds a third volume. The Register states the framework as a formal object rather than as an argument: one notation, six axioms and postulates, eighteen definitions, twenty-eight numbered results each carrying its hypotheses and its proof or citation, the certified numerical register, and the dependency graph. It introduces no new result and grades nothing higher than the corpus grades it elsewhere.

Its load-bearing contribution is the graph. Drawing every result as a node and every use as an edge gives 31 nodes and 33 edges with no cycles, grounded on exactly five roots: the two-sided reading, the definition of a record, the definition of the cluster projection, the unique order of the seven doings, and the minimal update. No claim in the framework is used in its own derivation. This rules out circularity completely and checkably; it does not establish that the claims are true, and the Register says so.

## Movement numbering, and the concordance

Inserting four movements shifted every movement from the tenth onward. Papers 18 and 19 were issued against the earlier numbering and are **preserved as issued** rather than silently edited, in keeping with the standing discipline that corrections belong in new printings and not in altered files. ## The single ledger

The corpus previously carried two open ledgers. It now carries one, at the end of the Monograph: one representation, one number that follows from it, one catalogue that follows from both, one combinatorial lemma, and three measurements. Every item states why it is open. The combinatorial lemma, that a complete carrier closure passes each of the 240 roots exactly once, is neither empirical nor representational and is the most attackable item on the list.

## Discipline
