# Unified Mechanics
### The Corpus · 2026 · Fifth Printing

Three core books and one casebook. One argument, told so it can be read, held to the measured record, and checked.

**Start with `00 The Corpus Atlas.pdf`.** It names the canonical files, the authority order, the reading routes, and where every result and every open question lives. It is short on purpose.

## The four books

**A. A World of Distinctions.** The continuous argument, twenty-seven movements. How a sentence, asked to describe itself, becomes a universe: the two channels, the golden proportion, the three weights, the price of crossing, the carrier it has to be written on, the descent that leaves only what can still be read, what survives that descent as space and as a clock, the shape spacetime is forced into, where gravity comes from, what it costs to be a thing at all, and a record that refuses to be overwritten. Read this one first, start to finish.

**B1. Universopedia, The Observational Standard.** The observational contract: what a record is, what may honestly be said about a system, and where established science ends and the framework's added layer begins.

**B2. Universopedia, Systems of Interpretation.** The casebook. The framework thrown at real systems, recording where it grips and where it slides off. The negative screens are kept deliberately and they reproduce.

**C. Unified Mechanics, The Formal Register.** The audit surface: one notation, numbered commitments, definitions and results, every constant in closed form, the dependency graph with its complete edge ledger, and the proof obligations with the reason each one stands. For checking rather than reading.

A combined reading edition of B1 and B2 is included and is named as one. It governs nothing the two canonical files do not.

## The grades

Every claim carries one: **PROVED**, **CONDITIONAL**, **PROPOSED**, **POSTULATE**, **OPEN**, or **DISSOLVED**. They are never mixed, and every open question states why it is open.

The argument is closed. That is checkable rather than asserted: the dependency graph shows that no result in the corpus depends on any open obligation, so the obligations are a forward work programme and not holes holding anything up.
