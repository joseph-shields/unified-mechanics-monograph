# Edition Note
### Corpus Edition 2026, Fifth Printing · 27–28 July 2026

The Fourth Printing closed the argument. This printing does two things: it attacks the proof obligations that argument left standing, and it repairs the edition so that the completion is visible rather than something a reader has to reconstruct.

---

## What moved in the theory

Five of the seven proof obligations moved, and they moved because the ledger was attacked from the wrong end on purpose.

The Register said most obligations waited on O1, the concrete representation of the cluster projection. That was true, and it is why nothing had shifted for two printings: every attempt went at O1 first and bounced. Coming in from O2 instead, a pure counting question, **determined** O1 rather than depending on it. The dependency ran backwards from what the ledger assumed.

**New results, carried by Paper 21 and numbered T29 to T32:**

- **T29.** Given T15 and T16, both already proved, "the spatial modes number three" is *identical* to "the geometry block has dimension five", and therefore to "the decayed part has rank three". A question about space became a question about one integer.
- **T30.** E8 has exactly three rank-three root subsystems up to Weyl conjugacy. Exhaustive, because the Weyl group is transitive on the 240 roots. All three leave a rank-five survivor, so the framework's most exposed number is robust against the choice.
- **T31.** Two of the three are forbidden by results the corpus already proves: one hosts no closed triangular history (T10), the other is reducible and so is two decay operators rather than one (T25). The survivor is unique. Its reduction is averaging over the Weyl group of the decayed subsystem; its gauge algebra is so(10); its matter sits in the spinor sixteen; and its family index splits one plus three, giving **three families**.
- **T32.** A complete carrier closure exists, by explicit construction, with a 240-step witness verified independently of the search that found it. The closure condition turned out to be free: the roots come in opposite pairs and sum to zero.

**Consequences.** O2 is closed conditionally. O3 is substantially answered, with chirality and anomaly freedom remaining. O4's existence half is proved. O1 is reduced to a single reading of A6's covariance clause, which is now the one load-bearing question in the representational half of the corpus: four separate debts collapsed into it. The standing vacuum readout now rests on a proved lemma rather than a granted one, and Paper 21 Section Nine reports its agreement with the measured cosmological constant at a fraction of a standard deviation with nothing fitted.

**Grades.** T29, T30 and T32 are PROVED. T31 is CONDITIONAL and says so in its statement. The corpus does not promote a reading by calling it natural.

Also added: `run_all.py`, which rebuilds every certificate and writes a run manifest with environment and hashes; `requirements.txt`; `data_manifest.csv` declaring every source with its licence; the Corpus Atlas; and the Papers Index, which explains the numbering gaps, the historical titles and what is superseded by what.

## The discipline, unchanged

Papers are preserved as issued. Corrections belong in new printings and in the Papers Index, never in altered files. Paper 19's title page still reads *The Five Coordinates*, and that mismatch is recorded rather than repaired. The Fourth Printing sits under `90 ARCHIVE`, byte-exact, its own frozen object. The negative screens are retained and reproduce.

## What was recommended and not done

A whole-corpus renaming to prefixed identifiers (UM-T24 and similar) was considered and rejected: it would churn cross-references in nine issued papers to solve an ambiguity that does not exist, since no other numbering in the corpus uses those forms. Tagged and accessible PDF output, and re-export of the Semantic Word System from source, remain outstanding and are recorded here rather than quietly dropped.
